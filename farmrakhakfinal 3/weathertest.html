Summer & Heatwave Protocol

गर्मी एवं लू से बचाव

  • Provide cool drinking water (below 20°C) with electrolytes.
  • Operate roof sprinklers / foggers during peak hours (12 PM - 3 PM).
  • Avoid feeding during the hottest part of the day; feed early morning.
🌧️

Monsoon & Humidity Protocol

मानसून व नमी प्रबंधन

  • Keep litter moisture below 25% by regular raking and adding lime powder.
  • Use anti-coccidial supplements in feed preventatively.
  • Inspect feed storage bags to prevent aflatoxin mold growth.
❄️

Winter & Cold Night Protocol

सर्दी व ठंड से बचाव

  • Maintain brooder heat (32°C in week 1, decreasing 3°C weekly).
  • Close side curtains during night while maintaining top ridge ventilation.
  • Increase feed energy density with vegetable oil/fats.
