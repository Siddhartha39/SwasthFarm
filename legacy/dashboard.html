📈 Productivity Tips for Livestock Farmers

1. Optimize Feeding Schedules

Feed animals during cooler morning/evening hours to improve digestion and nutrient absorption.

2. Regular Weekly Health Checks

Weekly health assessments detect early signs of fever or mastitis and prevent contagious spread.

3. Clean Drinking Water Chlorination

Provide clean fresh water chlorinated at 2-3 ppm. Dehydration immediately drops milk yield and egg laying.

4. Proper Shed Ventilation & Cooling

Ensure active airflow and foggers when THI exceeds 72 to prevent heat-stress milk drops.

5. Maintain Daily Digital Records

Track feed intake and yield in SwasthFarm to detect efficiency drops early.

📜 Government Livestock & Poultry Guidelines

1. Mandatory National Vaccination

Follow state veterinary schedules for FMD, HS-BQ (Cattle/Buffalo), CSF (Pig), and ND/LaSota (Poultry).

2. Strict Biosecurity & Vehicle Dipping

Restrict outside visitors and enforce vehicle wheel dip disinfection at farm entrance as per DAHD norms.

3. Safe Dead Bird / Animal Disposal

Dispose of deceased livestock by deep burial with limestone or incineration. Open dumping is strictly prohibited.

4. BIS-Approved Feed Standards

Ensure balanced cattle/poultry feed without fungal contamination. Store feed on raised wooden pallets.

5. Prompt Notifiable Disease Reporting

Report unusual mortality, Lumpy skin lumps, or Bird flu symptoms immediately to Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551).
